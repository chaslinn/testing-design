Logo, partners and imagery

Every brand asset in this system was copied from the live change-box.net site — no asset was redrawn or invented.

Logo

The brand mark is an inline lockup: a faceted isometric box symbol locked up beside the rounded geometric Change-Box wordmark. This replaces the older stacked symbol-over-wordmark mark that sat on a solid green block.

The mark

  • Box symbol (PNG asset): charcoal #272F3A on transparent — for white, paper and green backgrounds. Use the white-knockout PNG on ink / dark backgrounds. Never recolour, stroke or treat the box as a UI icon.
  • Wordmark: Nunito 900, letter-spacing -0.02em, line-height 1.
  • Lockup: horizontal flex row, vertically centred, gap 10–12 px. Glyph height ≈ wordmark cap height (0.9–1.05× font size). Never stack the glyph above the wordmark and never place the lockup on a solid coloured tile.

In-app symbol chips

Where the brand appears as an avatar or thumbnail (e.g. a "Change Box" message sender in the app inbox), the charcoal glyph sits centred inside a brand-green container:

  • Round avatar — green circle, glyph at ~50% of the circle's size.
  • Square thumbnail — 52 × 52 px, border-radius 13 px, green fill, glyph 26 px.

Photography vibe

Warm natural daylight; real UK families, children, fresh produce, grocery baskets; shallow depth-of-field; hopeful and candid — never stock-cheesy or stark "poverty" imagery. Colour grade is warm with greens and soft yellows.

Iconography

  • Lucide via CDN — clean, rounded, ~2px stroke — as a documented substitution for the site's own UI icons.
  • Sized 18–24px, currentColor, in ink or green.
  • No emoji in brand voice. Avoid Unicode symbol icons.
