Logo, partners and imagery

Every brand asset in this system was copied from the live change-box.net site — no asset was redrawn or invented.

Logo

The wordmark is a rounded geometric "Change-Box" lockup; the brand symbol is a faceted 3D box glyph in charcoal. Treat the box as the logo, not as a UI-icon style.

Partners

Eight partner logos appear as round/soft-square tiles in even rows on the marketing site.

GMCA · Livewell · TBBT · The Brick · University of Sheffield · ODI · Shopmate · N&N

Photography vibe

Warm natural daylight; real UK families, children, fresh produce, grocery baskets; shallow depth-of-field; hopeful and candid — never stock-cheesy or stark "poverty" imagery. Colour grade is warm with greens and soft yellows.

Iconography

  • Lucide via CDN — clean, rounded, ~2px stroke — as a documented substitution for the site's own UI icons.
  • Sized 18–24px, currentColor, in ink or green.
  • No emoji in brand voice. Avoid Unicode symbol icons.
