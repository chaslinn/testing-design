Typography

One humanist family — Nunito — covers display and body, echoing the rounded geometric wordmark of the logo while staying highly legible. JetBrains Mono is reserved for code and data.

Substitution flag: Nunito is the nearest widely-available match to the Change Box logo wordmark. The exact font from the live Wix site was not provided. Swap when the real brand fonts are supplied.

Display

Headings use Nunito 800 with tight tracking. Sentence case for body and most headings; Title Case only for brand mottos ("Impact First", "Joined Up Thinking").

Body

Body copy at Nunito 400/600, line-height 1.5. Voice is warm, plain-spoken, dignified — UK English throughout.

Tokens

--font-display "Nunito", system-ui, sans-serif
--font-body "Nunito", system-ui, sans-serif
--font-mono "JetBrains Mono", ui-monospace, monospace
