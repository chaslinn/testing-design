Spacing, radii and shadows

A 4-pixel base scale, generous white space, soft rounded everything, and warm low-spread shadows. Nothing is sharp-cornered; nothing is hard black.

Spacing scale

All spacing tokens are multiples of 4px. Use the larger steps for section padding and feature cards; the smaller steps for tight UI.

Radii and shadows

Inputs and cards use a soft 14px radius, feature cards 20–28px, pills and buttons fully rounded at 999px. Shadows are warm-tinted, low-spread, with a dedicated green glow under primary CTAs.

Tokens

--radius-sm 8px
--radius-md 14px  ·  inputs, list cards
--radius-lg 20–28px  ·  feature cards
--radius-pill 999px  ·  buttons, pills
--shadow-sm low elevation
--shadow-md card elevation
--shadow-brand green glow under primary CTAs

Motion

  • Restrained 150–250ms ease-out fades and slides.
  • Gentle press scale (≈0.97). No bounces.
  • Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
